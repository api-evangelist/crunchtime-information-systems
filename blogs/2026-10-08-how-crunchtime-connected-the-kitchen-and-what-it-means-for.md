Every restaurant kitchen runs on three kinds of information: what needs to get done, what needs to get prepped and accounted for, and how the shift performed. For years, each of those functions lived in its own isolated system, checked at its own time on separate tablets, clipboards, or back-office computers by whoever had a free minute to go look. Today, there's a better way.
